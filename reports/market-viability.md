# Prince Solar Hub: is it viable? (SOLAR-635)

Seen 2026-11-21. Scope: SunSynk owners in South Africa only. Research only; nothing changed in code, Paystack or ads. Second, deeper pass the same day: Facebook groups, 520 Google Play reviews, Google Trends, our own PostHog sign-up numbers, a fresh competitor sweep and the installer channel. Earlier detail: `reports/Prince Solar Hub competitors.md`, `research_notes/pvbiz competitor/`, `research_notes/pricing-2026-11/`.

## Are you wasting your time?

**Not as a side business. Yes, if you need it to replace a salary.**

For:
- Owners who find it convert well. Since tracking started (about 10 Nov): 119 showcase visitors, 23 sign-ups, 22 linked a SunSynk login, 5 opened checkout, 3 pay now.
- SunSynk's app is bad, and it's getting worse. 62% of its 520 Google Play reviews are 1-2 stars (39 of 59 in 2026). The top 2026 complaints are alerts that don't arrive, being logged out, and the app going offline. Our server-side alerts and clear screens fix the first two.
- Only one direct rival: PVBiz. No one else sells a SunSynk viewer to SA owners.
- Running cost is near zero. Past 100 plants, one payer in 30 covers everyone.

Against:
- The pool is small and has gone quiet. About 50,000-200,000 SunSynk homes. The SA owner Facebook group has 16,400 members but only 36 posts a month and 8 new members a week. The 37,000-member group has 28 posts a month and none new. Once load-shedding stopped, owners stopped looking at their systems.
- Every visitor so far came direct: no search traffic, no ads. That means your own network. Nobody has yet shown that strangers can be reached cheaply.
- Free is the norm. Owners who pay buy one-off hardware (Solar Assistant R3,399). PVBiz is cheaper per plant at list price, and most of its money comes from installers.
- No pure viewer app in SA has raised money. Hohm Energy ($8m) collapsed in 2024 when load-shedding eased.
- SunSynk can break access at any time. There's no API and no permission.

Realistic ceiling (estimate): 200-500 payers within 2 years, about R14,000-R35,000 a month net. 1,000+ payers (R70,000) needs a channel nobody has shown works yet.

Strongest case against this verdict: our conversion comes from 23 warm sign-ups, mostly people you know. It may not hold with strangers. The ceiling could be half this.

## What decides it (in 6 weeks, about R1,500)

1. **Strangers test.** Run the R1,500 ad test (E), and post once in SunSynk Users & Installers SA (public group, 4,600 members, allows answers in comments). Pass: under R300 per paying user. Fail: fewer than 2 payers.
2. **Owner calls.** 10 calls with people you don't know (D). Pass: 5 or more would pay at R49-R99.
3. **Free-week cohort.** Of the 15 sign-ups from 9-18 Nov, how many pay at day 7?
4. **Stop rule.** If 1 and 2 both fail, stop building features. Keep it running for the people who pay, and spend the time elsewhere.

## A. Market size (SunSynk, SA)

- About 675,000 SA homes had solar in 2025, up 86% in 3 years ([ITWeb](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/sa-partnership-plans-largest-residential-virtual-power-plant-in-africa/raYAyMorWPQ7J38N), citing BusinessDay; original not read). Over 90% of home systems sold include a battery: about 600,000 battery homes (estimate).
- SunSynk share of home hybrid inverters, retailer claims only, and they conflict: about 28% ([EnergyBee](https://energybee.co.za/brands/sunsynk)), about 25% ([EnergyBee](https://energybee.co.za/guides/sunsynk-vs-growatt-inverter-comparison-2026)), about 40% ([MacSell](https://macsellsolar.co.za/blogs/news/sunsynk-vs-luxpower-inverter-south-africa-2026-hardware-comparison)).
- One claim of "over 50,000 units sold in SA since 2020" ([SolarZA](https://solarza.co.za/articles/solar-inverter-reviews-south-africa)), unsourced and likely old.
- 25-40% of 600,000 gives 150,000-240,000 homes. The 50,000 claim is the floor. Working range: **50,000-200,000 SunSynk homes**.
- Sunsynk Connect has over 100,000 Google Play installs ([AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/dev/Sunsynk/)). Not split by country, but SA is SunSynk's home market.
- Growth has slowed: SA solar added 2.6 GW in 2023 and 1.6 GW in 2025, after load-shedding largely ended in 2024 ([pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/02/20/south-africa-adds-1-6-gw-of-solar-in-2025)). Sell to owners who already have a system.

Verdict: a real but mid-sized pool. No independent SunSynk count exists.

## D. Owner interview kit

Goal: 10-15 short calls (15 min) with SA SunSynk owners who are not friends or family. Brynne runs them.

Where to find them:
1. The 3 paying users and the free-week users: ask by email for 15 minutes. Best source; they already acted.
2. SunSynk threads on MyBroadband and PowerForum, and SunSynk owner Facebook groups: one post asking for 15-minute chats, after reading each group's rules.
3. Neighbours, estate WhatsApp groups, work colleagues with SunSynk.

Ask about what they did, not what they would do. Don't show the app until the end.

1. What SunSynk inverter and battery do you have? Who installed it, and when?
2. When did you last open the SunSynk app? What were you looking for?
3. Tell me about the last time something went wrong with the system. How did you find out?
4. What annoys you in the SunSynk app?
5. Have you tried anything else? PVBiz, Solar Assistant, Home Assistant, a spreadsheet? What did it cost?
6. Who else in the house looks at it?
7. Do you pay for any other monitoring: alarm, car tracker, armed response? How much?
8. (Last) Show the showcase. "Would you try this for a week?" Note the answer and whether they sign up that day.

Write down for each call: inverter model, how often they check, last problem, what they pay for add-ons, whether they know PVBiz, signed up yes/no. Five or more of 15 signing up that week is a strong signal; fewer than two is a weak one.

## E. Paid ad test (plan only, nothing spent)

- Budget: R1,500 over 10 days, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), South Africa, ages 30-65, interests SunSynk / solar power / home improvement.
- Two ads, R750 each, to see which pain sells:
  - "Know the moment the grid drops." (alerts)
  - "See where your solar went today." (clear daily view)
- Each ad links to the showcase with its own UTM tag, so PostHog splits sign-ups by ad.
- Measure: click rate, cost per sign-up, share who link a SunSynk login, share who pay after the 7-day week.
- Pass: cost per paying user under R300 (about 3 months of Home at R99). Fail: fewer than one payer from R1,500.
- Needs from Brynne: a Meta ad account on the company, the card, and a yes before any spend.

## G. SunSynk risk

- Terms of use: no SunSynk terms on third-party access found or readable. Unverified.
- No public SunSynk API or partner programme. Tools use the same login and endpoints as the app (api.sunsynk.net) ([GitHub](https://github.com/topics/sunsynkapi)).
- SunSynk "limits the number of API requests", number not given ([Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/sunsynk/)). The limit is per login; we use 5 requests per inverter each poll (2026-11-10).
- Breakages so far were login and auth changes, not a deliberate block: a new app version added an authorise step before login ([openHAB](https://community.openhab.org/t/new-sun-synk-connect-account-and-inverter-binding/155680)); a 2025 Home Assistant release logged in but saw no plants ([HA community](https://community.home-assistant.io/t/sunsynk-deye-integration-using-api/1010154)).
- SunSynk tolerates third parties for now: PVBiz runs a paid service on the same cloud, at installer scale.
- SunSynk's own paid tier (Sunsynk Saver, R109.99 on the ZA App Store) rates 1.3 stars in SA. It competes with us only on automation, which we don't do.
- Fallback: only local hardware (a logger or RS485 cable on the owner's network), as Solar Assistant and SI Gateway do. Not possible for a web-only app without shipping a device.

Verdict: the main risk is an undocumented, rate-limited, login-based API with no permission. No sign of SunSynk blocking anyone, and PVBiz has run on it since at least 2024. Keep polling light, and react fast to login changes.

## K. Installers

- 442 PV GreenCard-certified installer companies in SA ([PV GreenCard](https://pvgreencard.co.za/)). No SunSynk installer count is public.
- Most give monitoring free through Sunsynk Connect and sell a yearly service (R1,000-R3,000 a year, or "from R100 a month").
- No installer was found charging a monitoring-only fee.
- Installer pains: hanging portal, view-only accounts until SunSynk approves an upgrade, weak alerts, no fleet view.
- PVBiz (claims 1,000+ sites) and SOLARMAN Business already sell to installers, at quote-only prices.

Verdict: a real but small channel, with willingness to pay unproven. Talk to 3-5 installers before building anything for them.
