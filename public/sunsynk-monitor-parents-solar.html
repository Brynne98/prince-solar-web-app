Watch your parents’ solar.

Add your parents’ SunSynk system next to your own. See it live, get an email when their power goes, and spot a problem before they phone you. They share it as Visitor, so no password changes hands.

How to add it

  1. Ask them to share their plant in SunSynk Connect with admin@princesolarhub.co.za as Visitor. How to share.
  2. In Prince Solar Hub, choose Add plant, then Share plant, and type their plant ID.
  3. Their last 60 days come in, and Live starts at once.

What you see

  • Their solar, battery, home and grid, every minute.
  • Battery time left, so you know if they’ll get through the power cut.
  • An email when their power goes and when it’s back.
  • Panel checks, so a weak panel shows before the bill does.

Their system stays theirs

  • A Visitor can only look. Nothing you do can change their inverter.
  • They can remove the share in SunSynk Connect at any time. Prince Solar Hub stops reading at once.
  • Their password stays theirs. If they change it, nothing breaks.

Set up each plant

Each plant has its own Plant settings. Type their battery size and reserve once, and battery time left works for their house too.

Which plan

Home covers 2 plants: yours and theirs. Family covers 5, for the holiday house and the rest of the family.
