SunSynk Connect vs Prince Solar Hub

SunSynk Connect is SunSynk’s free app. It shows your system live and changes inverter settings. Prince Solar Hub reads the same data and is built for watching it: how long the battery will last, an email when the power goes, and faults found early. Many owners use both.

When SunSynk Connect is enough

You want to change settings, or you look now and then. It’s free and comes with your logger.

When Prince Solar Hub helps

You want to know if the battery lasts the night, hear about a power cut while you’re out, or see a weak panel before the bill does.

Questions

Can I use both?

Yes. Prince Solar Hub only reads your data. Keep SunSynk Connect for settings.

Do the numbers match?

Solar, home and battery match. With two or more inverters, our grid total can be higher: SunSynk counts the grid on one inverter, we count them all.

Is it faster than SunSynk Connect?

No. Both update every minute. The readings come from the same place.
