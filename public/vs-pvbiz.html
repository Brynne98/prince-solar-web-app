pvbiz vs Prince Solar Hub

pvbiz is a South African monitoring site that reads ten inverter brands, from R55 a month for 2 plants. Prince Solar Hub reads SunSynk only for now. It is free for one plant, and adds battery time left, panel checks and grid totals for every inverter.
