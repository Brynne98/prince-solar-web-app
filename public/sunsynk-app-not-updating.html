SunSynk app not updating?

Most of the time the data logger on your inverter has lost its Wi-Fi, so nothing reaches SunSynk. Check the logger first, then the app. Your inverter keeps running either way.

Why it happens

  • The logger is off Wi-Fi. The router restarted, the Wi-Fi password changed, or the signal near the inverter is weak.
  • The router has no power. In a power cut, a router not on backup takes the logger offline with it.
  • SunSynk is slow or down. Owners on the forums report it at the same time.
  • A second inverter lags. With two or more inverters, the second can send its readings only every 5 minutes.
  • The app is stuck. An old sign-in after an update.

What to do

  1. Open your plant in SunSynk Connect and look at the logger. Offline means the problem is at home.
  2. Check the logger’s light. Restart your router, wait 5 minutes, and look again.
  3. Changed your Wi-Fi name or password? Connect the logger to it again, as in SunSynk’s logger guide.
  4. Logger online but the app frozen? Sign out, clear the app’s data or reinstall it, and sign in again.
  5. Still stuck? Look on PowerForum or MyBroadband. If others report the same, it is SunSynk, and it comes back on its own.

How Prince Solar Hub helps

It reads from the same logger, so it can’t bring an offline logger back. It tells you sooner: each inverter shows when it last reported, and you get an email when nothing has come in for 30 minutes. When the logger is back, so are your readings.

Questions

Will the missing hours come back?

Usually not. If the logger was offline, it sent nothing, so there is nothing to fill the gap with.

Why are my two inverters out of step?

The second inverter can send its readings every 5 minutes, so it lags the first. That is how SunSynk works, and no setting in the app changes it.
