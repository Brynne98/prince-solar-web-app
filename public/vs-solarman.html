Solarman Smart vs Prince Solar Hub

Solarman Smart is the app for Solarman data loggers, found on many Deye and some SunSynk systems. Prince Solar Hub reads systems in the SunSynk app. Your logger decides which you can use: if your system shows in SunSynk Connect, it works here.

Which one you can use

It depends on the logger on your inverter. A SunSynk logger reports to SunSynk Connect. A Solarman logger reports to Solarman Smart. Prince Solar Hub reads SunSynk Connect, so it needs a system that shows there.

My system uses a Solarman logger. Will it work?

Not yet. Prince Solar Hub reads systems in the SunSynk app. Other brands are coming soon.

Which updates faster?

Solarman Smart updates every 5 minutes by default. Prince Solar Hub updates every minute.
