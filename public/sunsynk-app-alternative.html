A SunSynk app alternative.

Prince Solar Hub is a monitoring tool for SunSynk solar. It reads the same data as the SunSynk app and adds what owners ask for: how long the battery will last, an email when the power goes, checks on your panels, and your history kept. No extra hardware. Free for one plant.

What it adds

  • Battery time left. The time your battery reaches its reserve, updated every minute.
  • Email alerts. When the power goes and comes back, when panels stop, and when the battery runs hot. Pop-ups on your phone too.
  • Panel checks. Each string of panels against its usual share, so a weak one shows that day.
  • Grid for every inverter. With two or more inverters, SunSynk counts the grid on one. We count them all.
  • Rand saved. What your solar saved, from your own electricity rate.
  • Every day kept. On Home and Family, look back at any day, hour by hour, and download it to Excel.
  • Wall view. Full screen on a TV or tablet.
  • Every plant in one place. Your home, your parents’ and the holiday house.

What stays the same

  • Solar, home and battery match the SunSynk app.
  • It updates every minute, like the SunSynk app.
  • You keep the SunSynk app for settings. Prince Solar Hub only reads.

How to start

  1. Sign up with Google or your email.
  2. In SunSynk Connect, share your plant with admin@princesolarhub.co.za as Visitor. Or link your SunSynk login.
  3. Your last 60 days come in. Live starts at once.

Questions

Do I still need the SunSynk app?

Keep it for inverter settings. Prince Solar Hub only reads your data and never changes a setting.

Does it need extra hardware?

No. If your system shows in the SunSynk app, it works here.

What does it cost?

Free for one plant. Home is R99 a month for 2 plants. Family is R199 a month for 5.
