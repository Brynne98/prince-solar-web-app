8. How to ask for a record

  1. Fill in Form 2 of the PAIA Regulations, 2021. It is on the Information Regulator's website, or ask us for it.
  2. Say which record you want and how: a copy, by email, or to look at. If it is not about you, say which of your rights it helps protect.
  3. Email it to hello@princesolarhub.co.za, with a copy of your ID. If you ask for someone else, add proof that you may act for them.

We answer within 30 days. We may take 30 days more if the request is large, and we tell you if so. We may refuse a record on the grounds in Part 3, Chapter 4 of PAIA, such as another person's privacy or a company's confidential information. If we refuse, we say why.

9. Fees

Asking for your own personal information is free. For other records, the fees are those in the PAIA Regulations, 2021: a request fee, and an access fee for copying and finding the record. We tell you the amount before we start.

10. If you disagree

You can complain to the Information Regulator, or apply to a court, within 180 days of our answer.
