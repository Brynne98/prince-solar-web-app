SunSynk logger keeps going offline?

The logger is the small Wi-Fi stick on your inverter. When it drops off Wi-Fi, the SunSynk app goes quiet. A weak signal, a new router, or a router without backup power is the usual cause.

Why it happens

  • Weak signal. Inverters often sit in a garage or outside wall, far from the router.
  • The router changed. A new router, name or password, and the logger can’t find it.
  • The router lost power. In a power cut, a router not on backup switches off, and the logger with it.
  • The router restarts. Some routers restart at night or after updates, and the logger doesn’t always come back.

What to do

  1. Plug the router into a backed-up plug, or a small router UPS, so it stays on in a power cut.
  2. Test the signal: stand at the inverter with your phone. One bar or less is too weak.
  3. Add a Wi-Fi extender or mesh point near the inverter.
  4. After a router change, connect the logger again, as in SunSynk’s logger guide.
  5. Still dropping? A logger with a network cable port avoids Wi-Fi altogether.

Plant offline in the SunSynk app?

Offline means SunSynk has heard nothing from your logger for a while. The inverter still runs and still powers the house. Only the reporting stopped. Start with the router and the signal, as above.

SunSynk dongle not connecting to Wi-Fi?

The logger is also called the dongle or the Wi-Fi stick. If it won’t join after a router change, check you typed the new Wi-Fi name and password exactly. Some loggers only join 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, so pick that network if your router shows two.

Questions

Does the inverter keep working when the logger is offline?

Yes. The logger only sends readings. Your solar, battery and backup keep running.

Why does it go offline in a power cut?

The inverter keeps the logger powered, but your router may not be on backup. No router, no Wi-Fi, no readings.
