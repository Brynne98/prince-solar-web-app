Questions

Will it work with my inverter?

It works with SunSynk. If you see your system in the SunSynk app, it works here.

Can it change my settings?

No. It is read-only. It never changes your inverter settings.

Is my password safe?

We never keep your SunSynk password.

Do I need my installer?

Usually not. If your plant doesn't show in your SunSynk app, your installer still holds it and must share it with you first.

Is there a phone app?

Coming soon. Until then, add the website to your home screen and it opens like an app.

Can I choose which emails I get?

Yes. Every alert has its own switch, so you can turn off the ones you don't want.
