Questions

Will it work with my inverter?

Prince Solar Hub works with SunSynk inverters. If your system shows in the SunSynk app, it shows here too.

How often does it update?

Every minute, the same as the SunSynk app.

How far back does my history go?

When you connect, we bring in your last 60 days from SunSynk. From then on, Home and Family plans keep your full history.

What happens after the free week?

You keep the last 24 hours and every alert. Move to a Home or Family plan to keep your full history.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Your plan runs to your billing date, then moves to Free. Nothing more is charged.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time, from Account. Your logins, settings and history go with it, and billing stops. It cannot be undone.

Do my numbers match the SunSynk app?

Solar, home and battery match. With two or more inverters, our grid total can be higher: SunSynk counts the grid on one inverter, we count them all.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Pick any days, up to a year at a time, and download them from Account. The file opens in Excel or Google Sheets.

Is there a phone app?

Coming soon. Until then, add the website to your home screen and it opens like an app.
