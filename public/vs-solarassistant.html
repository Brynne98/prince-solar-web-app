SolarAssistant vs Prince Solar Hub

SolarAssistant runs on a Raspberry Pi wired to your inverter. It reads every 2 seconds, works without internet, and changes settings. It costs R999 for the software, or R2,499 to R3,399 with the Pi, once off. Prince Solar Hub needs no box or cable: it reads your system from SunSynk every minute.

Questions

Does Prince Solar Hub work when the internet is down?

No. It reads from SunSynk, so it needs your logger online. SolarAssistant keeps working at home without internet.

Can I use both?

Yes. SolarAssistant reads the inverter by cable and Prince Solar Hub reads SunSynk, so they don’t get in each other’s way.
