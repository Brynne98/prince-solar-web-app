Get an alert when the power goes.

The SunSynk app can send a push when the grid drops. Owners often miss it: notifications are off, or the phone closed the app. Prince Solar Hub emails you when the power goes and when it comes back.

Get the SunSynk push working

  1. Allow notifications for SunSynk Connect in your phone’s settings.
  2. Turn on the alerts in the app’s own notification settings.
  3. Android: take the app off battery saving, so the phone doesn’t close it.
  4. Test it at the next planned power cut.

Why owners still miss power cuts

  • The push is a single notice. Swipe it away and it’s gone.
  • Owners report that the “power back” notice doesn’t always come.
  • Only the phone with the app gets it. The rest of the house doesn’t.

How Prince Solar Hub helps

You get an email when the grid goes and another when it’s back, with how long it was off. Pop-ups on your phone or computer too, if you want them. The Grid page shades each power cut, so you can see the week at a glance. Battery time left tells you if you’ll make it through.

It needs your logger and router online. Put the router on backup power so the alert can reach you.

Questions

Does it work during load-shedding?

Yes, as long as your logger and router stay on backup power.

Can I turn some alerts off?

Yes. Switch off any alert you don’t want, by email or pop-up, in Account.
