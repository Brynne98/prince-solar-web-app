Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to Prince Solar Hub: the web app at app.princesolarhub.co.za and the website at princesolarhub.co.za (the "Service"). The website sets no cookies and sends no usage reports. The Service is run by Theron and Prince Solutions (Pty) Ltd, registration number 2026/182827/07, a company in South Africa ("we"). We are the responsible party for your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Our information officer is reached at the address under Contact.

We collect what the Service needs to show you your solar system, and nothing more. We do not sell your data, and the Service has no advertising.

Your account

When you create an account, we store:

  • Your email address.
  • Your password, if you sign up with email, stored only as a secure hash. We cannot read it.
  • Your IP address and browser type when you sign in, in the sign-in records.

Your email address is required. Without it there is no account.

Signing in with Google

If you choose Continue with Google, Google tells us your name, email address, whether Google has verified that email, and your profile picture link. We use them to create or find your account. We do not receive your Google password and we get no access to your Gmail, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your SunSynk login and plant

  • SunSynk login. We store your SunSynk username. We use your SunSynk password to get access tokens. We keep a refresh token, encrypted, and a short-lived access token. We note the time of each try to connect a login, to cap tries at five an hour, and delete it soon after the hour is up.
  • Plant details. Your plant's name, location, timezone and equipment, as SunSynk reports them.
  • Readings. Your inverter's readings, as often as SunSynk provides them, up to once a minute.
  • Paused plants. A plant your plan doesn't cover stays listed under your login, with its name and the login's tokens, but we save no readings for it. Remove the login to delete them.
  • Payments. On a paid plan, Paystack takes your card details; we never see the card number. We keep what Paystack gives us to charge the same card again: the card type, its last four digits and expiry, and a charge code. We also keep each payment's amount, date and Paystack reference, and show you the card and your last payments in Account. Changing your card takes R1 on the new card, which Paystack pays straight back.
  • Settings. Settings you enter, such as your electricity tariff.

A SunSynk login is required. Without it there is no dashboard. Settings are optional.

A plant's readings are visible only to accounts linked to that plant. If someone else links the same plant with a SunSynk login, they see the same readings.

Usage and error reports

The app tells us when you sign up, link a plant, open a page or use a feature, such as saving a setting. Each report carries the time, the page, the app version, your browser type, your IP address, your account ID and your plant ID. Never your email, name, SunSynk login or readings. We use them to see which parts of the app are used and where people get stuck.

When the app hits an error, it also sends the error text and its technical trace. We use them only to fix the app.

Both go to PostHog. The app stores nothing on your device for this: no cookies.

Emails

We send email to the address on your account:

  • Account emails: sign-up confirmation, password reset, password changed, welcome, history imported, SunSynk login needs reconnecting, account deleted. These always go out.
  • Alerts: grid off and back, battery hot, batteries out of step, a panel string or the inverter not reporting, SunSynk down and back.
  • Reports: weekly, monthly and yearly.

Alerts and reports carry your plant's name and readings. Turn each one off in the app (Account, Notifications) or with the unsubscribe link in the email.

Pop-up alerts

The same alerts can pop up on a browser, once you turn them on there (Account, Notifications, On this browser). Nothing asks before you do. Turning them on stores that browser's push address and its keys, so we can send to it. Each pop-up carries the alert's words, your plant's name and which page to open. It goes through your browser maker's push service (Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari, Mozilla for Firefox), encrypted so only your browser can read it.

Turning pop-ups off, or signing out, deletes that browser's address. So does deleting your account. Alerts shown in the app's list are the ones we already send by email, kept 30 days in the list.

Who receives your data

  • Supabase stores the database and handles sign-in.
  • Infobip delivers sign-up, password reset and password changed emails. It gets your email address and the email.
  • Resend delivers every other email. It gets your email address and the email.
  • Cloudflare serves the app.
  • Google Fonts, unpkg and jsDelivr serve fonts and code the app loads. They see your IP address and browser, as any website you load them from does.
  • Google handles Google sign-in, only if you choose it.
  • Paystack takes payments on a paid plan. It gets your card details, email address and the amount.
  • PostHog stores the usage and error reports, in the EU.
  • Google, Apple or Mozilla deliver pop-up alerts, only to a browser where you turned them on. They get the encrypted pop-up.
  • SunSynk sends your readings when we ask with your token.

Each receives only what its job needs.

Where your data is kept

The database is hosted by Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany. EU data protection law (GDPR) protects it there to a standard like POPIA. PostHog keeps the usage and error reports in the EU too. Cloudflare, Google, Apple, Mozilla, Infobip, Resend, Paystack, unpkg, jsDelivr and SunSynk may process data in other countries under their own safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Readings: minute readings for 31 days, then as 5-minute averages for as long as your account is open.
  • Your account: for as long as it is open. You can delete it in the app (Account, Delete account). That deletes your login, your SunSynk logins and tokens, and your link to the plant. If no one else is linked to the plant, its readings and settings are deleted too; if someone else is, they stay for that person.
  • Usage and error reports: up to one year. After you delete your account, a report keeps the account ID and plant ID, but nothing links that ID to you.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in database backups until those backups are replaced.

Security

Traffic to the Service is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed, SunSynk refresh tokens are encrypted, and each plant's readings are visible only to the accounts linked to it. Minute-level readings can show when a home is occupied, so we treat them as personal information. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your personal information we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Children

The Service is for adults. It is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its processing. You can delete your account in the app yourself. For anything else, email us. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): https://inforegulator.org.za, POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za
